Kiomon is a **context engine for AI agents**. It lets you save, forage, and organize information from the web — then gives Claude, Cursor, and any MCP-compatible agent persistent access to that knowledge base.

Think of it as **long-term memory for your AI agents**: a brain your assistant can read, search, and write to, that survives across sessions, projects, and tools.

## What you get

<CardGrid>
	<Card title="Save anything you read">
		Capture full articles, highlighted text, and URLs with the browser extension — or feed your library from Notion, Confluence, and Google Drive.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Find things by keyword and meaning">
		Kiomon's hybrid search combines exact keyword matching with semantic understanding, so you find things by phrase *and* by concept.

	</Card>

	</Card>

## How it works

<Steps>
	1. **Capture** — Save pages, highlights, and notes with the browser extension, or sync a connected source (Notion, Google Drive, Confluence).
	2. **Organize** — Kiomon auto-tags every document, extracts the ideas it contains, and links related items in your knowledge graph.
	3. **Retrieve** — Ask your agent anything. It searches your library with hybrid search and answers with citations.
	4. **Remember** — Your agent reflects at the end of sessions; high-confidence memories auto-activate through the write gate, while lower-confidence drafts wait in your Review Inbox.
</Steps>
