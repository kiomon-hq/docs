# Security policy

## What this repository is

This repository publishes the Kiomon documentation site ([docs.kiomon.com](https://docs.kiomon.com)).
It contains documentation, brand assets, and the static site's build configuration — no product
source code, no customer data, and no credentials.

A finding here is therefore most likely about **the site itself**: its dependencies, its client-side
code, or something it serves. Vulnerabilities in the Kiomon product, API, MCP endpoint or SDKs are
also welcome through the same channel; they are routed to the team that owns them.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please report privately. **Do not open a public issue, pull request or discussion for a
vulnerability** — a public report is a disclosure before there is a fix.

- Use the **Security report** category on the [contact form](https://kiomon.com/contact), or
- email [support@kiomon.com](mailto:support@kiomon.com) with `security` in the subject line.

A useful report says:

- what the issue is, and the impact you believe it has;
- where it is — the URL, the repository path, or the request you sent;
- the shortest reproduction you have, and whether it needs an account;
- any safe contact so a fix can be confirmed with you.

Please do not include third-party personal data, and do not access, change or retain other people's
data while testing.

## What happens next

Reports are read by the team that owns the affected surface. Kiomon will tell you what it could
reproduce, what it assessed, and when a fix ships. Credit in the fix's notes is offered — say in the
report whether you want it.

## Scope and good faith

In scope: this repository, and the site served from `docs.kiomon.com`.

Out of scope: findings that require a compromised device or browser, findings in third-party
services that Kiomon merely links to, and reports generated by a scanner with no demonstrated
impact.

Research done in good faith — on your own account, without degrading the service for others, and
disclosed privately first — is welcome, and Kiomon will not pursue action over it.
